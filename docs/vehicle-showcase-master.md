# Vehicle showcase sheet — master specification

Status: **MASTER TEMPLATE**
Reference vehicle: **Veloce Juanicar C2.20**
Established: 2026-09-28

This document defines the visual sheet to use for every current and future Top Down RACE: CraftRace vehicle. The Juanicar C2.20 sheet approved on 2026-09-28 is the visual reference.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Use the real in-game vehicle asset as the primary design reference. Do not redesign the car.
- Use the official fictional manufacturer identity from the game. Never invent or substitute a real-world manufacturer badge.
- Manufacturer badge on the vehicle must use the **short emblem only** (for Veloce: the V/shield mark), not the long wordmark.
- No license plates unless explicitly requested.
- Preserve body shape, paint/livery, roof, glass, wheels and distinguishing details of the source asset.
- Do not invent model suffixes, specifications or performance values. Read them from game data when available.
- Keep a consistent 16:9 presentation and the same visual hierarchy across the whole fleet.

## Composition

1. Manufacturer logo/wordmark + large model name in the upper-left.
2. Large 3/4 front hero render as the dominant image.
3. Performance panel at left: Acceleration, Top Speed, Handling, Braking.
4. Orthographic strip: front, rear and side.
5. Bottom strip: top-down front/rear plus several 3/4 top-down angles suitable for comparison with the in-game sprite.
6. Top Down RACE: CraftRace branding in the lower-right.

## Art direction

Dark premium racing-game studio/garage; black/charcoal base; cool cyan/blue accents balanced with restrained warm red/orange practical lights; reflective floor; strong but controlled rim lighting. Vehicle remains the focal point. Photoreal/polished 3D presentation while preserving the source vehicle's identity.

## Production rule

Generate/review **one vehicle at a time**. Compare each finished sheet against both its original in-game asset and the Juanicar master sheet before accepting it. Consistency must never come at the cost of changing the car's actual design.
